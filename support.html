Getting started

  1. Add your shifts. In Shifts, import your roster from your calendar or an .ics file, set up a repeating rotation (for example 3 nights on, 2 off), or add single shifts.
  2. Open Today. Your plan shows when to sleep, when to get bright light, when to keep light dim, and your last caffeine time. Tap the Circadian Map for details.
  3. Log as you go. Use the camera button to snap a meal or coffee, or tap one of Your Usuals to log it in one tap.

Common questions

My wake alarm didn't make a sound

Circarest's alarms are notifications, so they can't ring through the silent switch. Before you sleep:

  • Keep the ringer on (the Ring/Silent switch, or the Action button on iPhone 15 Pro and later).
  • Check Settings › Notifications › Circarest: allow notifications, turn on Sounds and Time-Sensitive Notifications.
  • If you use a Focus while sleeping, allow time-sensitive notifications in that Focus.

Alarms repeat twice unless you tap I'm up.

Changing or deleting a shift

Tap a shift in Shifts to change its type, date, start time, length or note, or to delete it. You can also swipe left on a shift to delete it, and undo straight after. If you change a shift that came from your calendar, it's unlinked from the calendar so a sync doesn't change it back.

If a new shift overlaps one you already have, Circarest tells you which. Choose Add Anyway to keep both: they're listed in the order they start, and your plan treats them as one stretch of work.

Setting up 2 on, 2 off (or 3 on, 3 off)

In Shifts, tap Set Up Rotation. Under Days on, days off, pick day, night or evening shifts and a pattern such as 2 & 2, 3 & 3 or 4 & 3, or set your own number of working days and days off. Then adjust the start time and length in the cycle below and tap Apply.

How does Circarest decide when I should sleep?

It works out your sleep from your shifts, commute and sleep need. After a night shift it plans recovery sleep after you get home. Before a first night shift it suggests a nap. After your last night it can plan a short sleep and a normal bedtime, so you switch back to days faster (you can turn this off in Settings). The plan is general wellness guidance based on published research, not medical advice.

Importing my roster from the calendar

In Shifts, tap the calendar button, allow calendar access and choose the calendar your work roster is in. Events of 3–28 hours count as shifts; cancelled events are skipped. You can add a keyword (for example "Shift") to ignore other events. Circarest only reads your work calendar; it never changes it. (Plan in Calendar, if you turn it on, writes only to its own “Circarest Plan” calendar.) Shifts you delete stay deleted, even if they're still in your calendar.

Connecting Apple Health, Apple Watch, WHOOP or Oura

Go to Settings › Health & Devices › Connect Apple Health. Trackers like WHOOP and Oura share sleep and heart data through Apple Health, so turn on Apple Health sharing in their own apps first. Circarest then uses your measured sleep for check-ins, your weekly review and the Drive Check.

What does the Drive Check do?

It combines how long you've been awake, your sleep, the time of day, how sleepy you feel, and an optional 90-second reaction test, based on published fatigue research. Results are saved with their date so you can compare them in Settings › Drive check history.

It can't tell you you're safe to drive. If you feel drowsy, don't drive: take a short nap, get a lift or use public transport.

Red night mode and light mode

Red night mode turns the whole screen dim red to protect your night vision. By default it switches on automatically in the evening; change this in Settings › Display. You can also choose a Dark, Light or System appearance there. Red night mode takes over from both when it's on.

Widgets, Lock Screen and Live Activities

Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, and add the Circarest widget to see your current phase, last caffeine and next sleep. During a shift, a Live Activity shows a countdown on the Lock Screen, and in the Dynamic Island on iPhones that have one. Widgets and the Live Activity are part of Circarest Plus. If you don't see it, check Settings › Circarest › Live Activities.

Siri and the Action button

Try "Log my usual in Circarest", "When's my caffeine cutoff in Circarest", "When do I sleep in Circarest" or "Start a nap in Circarest". On iPhones with an Action button (iPhone 15 Pro and later), you can open the camera with it: choose Shortcut › Circarest › Snap Food or Coffee in Settings › Action Button.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. In Settings › Privacy, turn on Lock with Face ID (or Touch ID). Circarest then asks for Face ID, or your iPhone passcode as a backup, every time you open it. Ask again sets how long it can stay in the background first. Reminders, alarms and widgets still work on the Lock Screen.

How do I share Circarest with a colleague?

Tap Share Circarest in Settings › About Circarest, at the end of Your Week, or after a Drive Check. It opens the normal iPhone share sheet with a short message and the App Store link. We don't see who you share with.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Go to Settings › About Circarest › Export My Data. Circarest makes one file (Circarest-Export-date.json) with your profile, shifts, rotations, log, check-ins and drive checks, and opens the share sheet so you can save it to Files, AirDrop it or email it to yourself. Dates are in standard ISO 8601 form and measurements are metric. Meal photos aren't included.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe left on any shift, log entry or drive check to delete it. To remove everything, go to Settings › Erase All My Data: it removes your shifts, log, drive checks, profile and reminders, and starts the app fresh. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone, but not the “Circarest Plan” calendar if you turned on Plan in Calendar: turn that off first, or delete the calendar in the Calendar app. Your data is never stored on our servers, so there's nothing else to delete. See the Privacy Policy.

What's in Circarest Plus, and how do I cancel?

Circarest Plus adds Learns Your Sleep, sleep debt and recovery, your plan in Apple Calendar, Snap photo recognition, Your Week insights, Apple Health and wearables, your personal health plan, widgets and the Live Activity, schedule import from your calendar, and 3 years of history instead of 6 months. It's $5.99 a month or $39.99 a year (US prices), with no free trial. It renews automatically until you cancel. Drive Check, wake alarms, the nap planner and your sleep, light and caffeine plan are free for everyone.

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To cancel, go to Settings › Circarest Plus › Manage Subscription in the app, or Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone, at least 24 hours before it renews. On a new iPhone, tap Restore Purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple: see the Refund Policy.

How much history does Circarest keep?

6 months of your log, check-ins, Drive Check results and past shifts, and 90 days of meal photos. Circarest Plus keeps 3 years of history and a year of photos. Older items are removed automatically so the app stays fast; export your data from Settings › About Circarest › Export My Data to keep a copy. To keep things quick, a day can hold up to 60 log entries and 20 Drive Check results.

Ounces and pounds, or milliliters and kilograms?

Circarest follows your iPhone's region: in the US you'll see fluid ounces, ounces, pounds, feet and inches, and Calories. To change it, go to Settings › Display › Units and choose US or Metric.

Does Circarest work offline?

Yes. Everything runs on your iPhone, including meal and coffee recognition. No internet connection is needed.
