Accessibility

Circarest app and website · Last reviewed 28 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use Circarest. We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

This website

  • Text and links meet WCAG AA colour contrast in light and dark mode.
  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Pages use headings, lists and landmarks so screen readers can move around them.
  • Text resizes with your browser settings, and pages reflow on small screens without scrolling sideways.
  • No time limits, flashing content, auto-playing media or pop-ups.

The app

The Circarest app supports VoiceOver, larger text sizes, light and dark appearance, and Reduce Motion.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this website or in the app is hard to use, email hello@5hz.io. Tell us the page or screen and what happened, and we'll help you get the information another way and work on a fix.
