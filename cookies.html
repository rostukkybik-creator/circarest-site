We don't use cookies

This website doesn't set cookies or use similar technologies such as local storage, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or analytics. That's why there's no cookie banner: there's nothing to agree to.

This website

  • The pages are plain HTML and CSS, with no scripts.
  • They don't load anything from other companies: no fonts, videos, maps, social media buttons, ads or analytics.
  • Our host, GitHub Pages, doesn't set cookies on these pages. It does receive the technical details your browser sends with each request, as any web server does. See the Privacy Policy.

The app

The Circarest app doesn't use cookies, advertising identifiers or tracking technologies of any kind.

Other websites

Sites we link to, such as apple.com, github.com and 5hz.io, may use their own cookies under their own policies.

If this changes

If we ever add cookies that aren't strictly necessary, we'll update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it before setting them.
