Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Circarest app and website · Last updated 28 October 2026

This policy is required by Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's SB 370 and similar state laws. It covers the Circarest iPhone app, this website and messages you send to 5Hz development agency ("we", "us"), the developer of Circarest.

Consumer health data we collect, and why

In the app

The app works with the following only on your iPhone. It's never sent to us or anyone else.

  • Sleep, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, exercise, workouts, time in daylight, height, weight, date of birth and sex, if you connect Apple Health.
  • Body details you enter: birth year, sex, height, weight, neck size, pregnancy, hormonal contraception, smoking, and sleep apnea questionnaire answers.
  • What you log: drinks and caffeine, meals, workouts, supplements and sleep check-ins.
  • Drive Check results: reaction times, how sleepy you felt, hours of sleep and hours awake.

Purpose: to give you the features you use, such as your sleep plan, caffeine timing, insights, reminders and checks.

When you contact us

If you choose to include health information in an email to us, we receive it and use it only to reply to you.

Where it comes from

  • You.
  • Apple Health, and the devices and apps that share data with it, if you connect it.

Consumer health data we share

None. We don't sell consumer health data or share it with any third party or affiliate. Emails you send us are stored by the provider that hosts our email, which processes them only on our behalf.

Third parties and affiliates we share it with

None.

Your rights

  • Confirm and access: ask whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and get a copy of any we hold.
  • Third parties: ask for a list of the third parties and affiliates we've shared it with (there are none).
  • Delete: ask us to delete it. In the app, you can delete entries, use Settings › Erase All My Data, or delete the app.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off Apple Health, Calendar or Camera access for Circarest in the iPhone's Settings app at any time.

To make a request, email hello@5hz.io with the subject "Health data request". We'll respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we'll tell you why within those 45 days, and take no more than 45 days more.

Appeals

If we turn down your request, reply to our answer with the subject "Appeal". We'll respond within 45 days and explain our decision. If your appeal is denied, Washington residents can contact the Washington State Attorney General, and residents of other states can contact their state Attorney General.
