Privacy Policy

Circarest app and website · Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version

  • Your app data stays on your iPhone. We don't run any server for the app, and we never receive your shifts, sleep, health, food or drive-check data.
  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Neither the app nor this website contains advertising, analytics or tracking code, and neither uses cookies.
  • Apple Health is read-only. Circarest never writes to Apple Health and never uses health data for advertising.
  • If you email us, we get your email address and what you write, and use it only to reply.
  • You're in control. You can export everything as one file (Settings › Export My Data), delete any entry, or erase everything at once (Settings › Erase All My Data).

Who we are

Circarest is developed by 5Hz development agency ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Circarest iPhone app, this website, and messages you send us. Questions about privacy: hello@5hz.io.

The app

What Circarest stores, and where

Circarest never stores your data on any server, and never in iCloud, with one exception you choose: if you turn on Plan in Calendar, your planned sleep, nap and last-caffeine times go into the “Circarest Plan” calendar in your own calendar account, which may be iCloud. Everything below is stored only in the app's private storage on your device, which iOS encrypts when your phone is locked. It is included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or your computer) according to your Apple settings. We can't access those backups.

Apple Health (optional)

If you connect Apple Health, Circarest reads:

  • sleep;
  • heart-rate variability and resting heart rate;
  • exercise minutes and workouts;
  • time in daylight;
  • height, weight, date of birth and sex.

This data can come from your iPhone, Apple Watch or other devices that share with Apple Health. It's used only on your device, to adjust your plan, check your sleep against it, show recovery and daylight, and pre-fill body details if you ask. Circarest never writes to Apple Health. Health data is never shared, sold, or used for advertising or marketing. You can change or revoke access at any time in the iPhone's Settings app under Privacy & Security › Health.

Calendar (optional)

If you import your roster from your calendar, Circarest reads events from the calendars you choose, looking up to eight weeks ahead, to find your shifts. It never creates, changes or deletes events in those calendars.

If you turn on Plan in Calendar (Circarest Plus), Circarest creates one calendar of its own, called “Circarest Plan”, and keeps your planned sleep, naps and last-caffeine times in it for the coming week. Only that calendar is written to. It lives in the calendar account your iPhone uses by default (often iCloud), so you can share it with a partner from the Calendar app. It contains plan times only: no health data. Turning the feature off, or Erase All My Data, deletes it.

Camera and photos (optional)

When you snap a meal or drink, the photo is analyzed on your iPhone using Apple's built-in image recognition. Photos are never uploaded. If you log the item, a small copy of the photo is saved with that entry inside the app. When you choose a photo from your library, iOS gives Circarest only that one photo, not your library.

Notifications (optional)

Reminders and wake alarms are scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

Features that involve Apple

  • Siri and Shortcuts. When you use a Circarest action with Siri, Apple processes your voice request under Apple's Privacy Policy. The action itself runs on your device.
  • Widgets and Live Activities. A summary of your plan (for example your next sleep and last caffeine time) is shared with Circarest's widgets on your device. These can appear on your Lock Screen, where anyone holding your phone can see them, unless App Lock is on. You can remove them at any time.
  • Circarest Plus subscriptions. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email, card or other payment details. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple Account has an active subscription, and Apple handles purchases, renewals, refunds and cancellations under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Sharing Circarest. “Share Circarest” and “Share My Sleep Times” use the iOS share sheet. The message goes straight from your iPhone to the app you pick. We never see who you share with, and there are no referral codes or tracking links.
  • App Lock. If you turn on App Lock, Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode is checked by iOS on your device. Circarest only learns whether it succeeded, never your face, fingerprint or passcode.
  • Crash reports. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you. You can turn this off at any time.
  • Diagnostic reports on your iPhone. iOS gives Circarest reports about crashes and slow-downs (Apple's MetricKit). The app keeps the 10 newest on your iPhone and never sends them anywhere. They contain the app version, device type and technical details of the problem, not your health data or anything you've entered. They reach us only if you switch on Attach Diagnostic Reports in Settings › Email Support and send the email yourself.

How long app data is kept

Circarest keeps 6 months of history on your device: your log, check-ins, Drive Check results and past shifts. With Circarest Plus it keeps 3 years. Meal photos are kept for 90 days (a year with Plus); the meal itself stays. Older items are deleted automatically, about once a day, and there are daily limits on how much can be added (for example 60 log entries a day), so the app stays fast. If a Plus subscription ends, Plus history is kept for another 30 days first. You can take a copy at any time with Settings › Export My Data. You can swipe to delete shifts and log entries, delete individual Drive Check results, or remove everything at once with Settings › Erase All My Data. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Copies in your own device backups follow your backup settings. Export files are deleted as soon as the share sheet closes. If you turned on Plan in Calendar, the “Circarest Plan” calendar stays in your calendar account until you turn the feature off, erase your data, or delete the calendar yourself; deleting the app alone doesn't remove it.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write, including any health details you choose to share. Emails written from Settings › Email Support also include your iPhone model, iOS version and Circarest version, and diagnostic reports if you choose to attach them. We use it only to reply and help you. It's stored by the provider that hosts our email, on our behalf. We don't sell or share it, don't add you to any mailing list, and delete it when it's no longer needed to help you, or sooner if you ask. If you use the contact form on 5hz.io instead, that site's privacy policy applies.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Neither the app nor this website tracks you across other sites or apps, and we don't sell or share personal information. So we treat everyone the same, whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

Your rights elsewhere

Where laws such as the GDPR and UK GDPR apply, you have rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to processing. App data is under your control in the app; for anything you've sent us, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Circarest is made for adults 18 and over. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

App data is kept in the app's private storage, encrypted by iOS while your iPhone is locked, and you can add App Lock. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your iPhone protected with a passcode.

Health information

Circarest provides lifestyle and circadian suggestions for general wellness. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. The Drive Check supports your own judgement. It can't guarantee you are safe to drive and isn't a medical or legal assessment.
