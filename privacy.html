Privacy Policy

Circarest for iPhone · Effective 14 October 2026

The short version

  • Your data stays on your iPhone. We don't run any server for the app, and we never receive your shifts, sleep, health, food or drive-check data.
  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
  • Apple Health is read-only. Circarest never writes to Apple Health and never uses health data for advertising.
  • You're in control. You can export everything as one file (Settings › Export My Data), delete any entry, or erase everything at once (Settings › Erase All My Data).

Who we are

Circarest is made by 5Hz development agency ("we", "us"). Questions about privacy: hello@5hz.io, or 5hz.io/contact.

What Circarest stores, and where

Circarest never stores your data on any server, and never in iCloud, with one exception you choose: if you turn on Plan in Calendar, your planned sleep, nap and last-caffeine times go into the “Circarest Plan” calendar in your own calendar account, which may be iCloud. Everything below is stored only in the app's private storage on your device, which iOS encrypts when your phone is locked. It is included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or your computer) according to your Apple settings. We can't access those backups.

Permissions you can choose to give

Apple Health (optional)

If you connect Apple Health, Circarest reads:

  • sleep;
  • heart-rate variability and resting heart rate;
  • exercise minutes and workouts;
  • time in daylight;
  • height, weight, date of birth and sex.

This data can come from your iPhone, Apple Watch or other devices that share with Apple Health. It's used only on your device, to adjust your plan, check your sleep against it, show recovery and daylight, and pre-fill body details if you ask. Circarest never writes to Apple Health. Health data is never shared, sold, or used for advertising or marketing. You can change or revoke access at any time in the iPhone's Settings app under Privacy & Security › Health.

Calendar (optional)

If you import your roster from your calendar, Circarest reads events from the calendars you choose, looking up to eight weeks ahead, to find your shifts. It never creates, changes or deletes events in your calendars.

If you turn on Plan in Calendar (Circarest Plus), Circarest creates one calendar of its own, called “Circarest Plan”, and keeps your planned sleep, naps and last-caffeine times in it for the coming week. Only that calendar is written to. It lives in the calendar account your iPhone uses by default (often iCloud), so you can share it with a partner from the Calendar app. It contains plan times only: no health data. Turning the feature off, or Erase All My Data, deletes it.

Camera and photos (optional)

When you snap a meal or drink, the photo is analyzed on your iPhone using Apple's built-in image recognition. Photos are never uploaded. If you log the item, a small copy of the photo is saved with that entry inside the app. When you choose a photo from your library, iOS gives Circarest only that one photo, not your library.

Notifications (optional)

Reminders and wake alarms are scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

Features that involve Apple

  • Siri and Shortcuts. When you use a Circarest action with Siri, Apple processes your voice request under Apple's Privacy Policy. The action itself runs on your device.
  • Widgets and Live Activities. A summary of your plan (for example your next sleep and last caffeine time) is shared with Circarest's widgets on your device. These can appear on your Lock Screen, where anyone holding your phone can see them. You can remove them at any time.
  • Circarest Plus subscriptions. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email, card or other payment details. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple Account has an active subscription, and Apple handles purchases, renewals, refunds and cancellations under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Sharing Circarest. “Share Circarest” and “Share My Sleep Times” use the iOS share sheet. The message goes straight from your iPhone to the app you pick. We never see who you share with, and there are no referral codes or tracking links.
  • App Lock. If you turn on App Lock, Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode is checked by iOS on your device. Circarest only learns whether it succeeded, never your face, fingerprint or passcode.
  • Crash reports. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. They don't identify you, and you can turn this off at any time.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, receive or store your personal data on any server.
  • We don't sell or share data with anyone, including advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't track you or build a profile of you.
  • We don't use your data to train AI models.

How long data is kept

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can take a copy at any time with Settings › Export My Data. You can swipe to delete shifts and log entries in the app, delete individual Safe to Drive results, or remove everything at once with Settings › Erase All My Data. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Copies in your own device backups follow your backup settings. Export files are deleted as soon as the share sheet closes. If you turned on Plan in Calendar, the “Circarest Plan” calendar stays in your calendar account until you turn the feature off, erase your data, or delete the calendar yourself.

US privacy rights

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (Washington, Nevada and similar laws)

Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's SB 370 and similar state laws protect "consumer health data". Here's how Circarest handles it:

  • Consumer health data in the app:
    • sleep and heart data you allow from Apple Health;
    • body details and sleep apnea questionnaire answers;
    • meals, caffeine, workouts and supplements you log;
    • Safe to Drive results.
  • How it's used: only on your device, to give you the app's features: your plan, insights, reminders and checks.
  • Where it comes from: you, and Apple Health if you connect it.
  • Collection and sharing: we, the developer, don't collect, receive, sell or share it with anyone, including third parties or affiliates. It never leaves your device to us.
  • No geofencing: we don't use location or geofencing.
  • Your rights: you can confirm we hold no data, delete data in the app (or delete the app), and withdraw consent at any time by revoking Apple Health, Calendar or Camera access in iOS Settings. If a request is ever refused, you can appeal by contacting us, and for Washington residents, by contacting the Washington State Attorney General.

Other US states

Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws (for example Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas and Oregon) have similar rights. The same answer applies: we don't collect, sell or share your personal data, and you control everything in the app.

HIPAA

Circarest is a consumer wellness app, not a service of your employer or health care provider. We're not a HIPAA covered entity or business associate, and the app doesn't send data to either.

Your rights elsewhere

In the EU, UK and other regions (for example under the GDPR and UK GDPR), you have rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to processing. Because we don't collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing we can retrieve or delete on our side: everything is under your control in the app. If you have a question or complaint, contact us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Circarest is made for adults who work shifts. It isn't directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Health information

This app provides lifestyle and circadian suggestions for general wellness and is not a regulated medical device or treatment for sleep disorders. The Safe to Drive check supports your own judgement. It can't guarantee you are safe to drive and isn't a medical or legal assessment.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Circarest handles data, we'll update this page and the effective date above, and explain any important change in the app or its release notes before it applies.
