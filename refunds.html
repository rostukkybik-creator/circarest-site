Refund Policy

Circarest Plus · Last updated 29 October 2026

Refunds are handled by Apple

Circarest is free to download. Circarest Plus is sold and billed by Apple through the App Store, so Apple processes every payment and decides every refund under its own policies. We never receive your payment details and can't issue refunds ourselves.

Request a refund from Apple

How to ask for a refund

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe.
  2. Choose to request a refund, then pick Circarest Plus and tell Apple why.
  3. Apple will let you know what it decides.

Cancelling

The subscription renews automatically until you cancel. To cancel, open Settings › Circarest Plus › Manage Subscription in the app, or Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone, at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep Circarest Plus until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app doesn't cancel the subscription.

Need help?

If something isn't working, email hello@5hz.io and we'll try to fix it. Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the law.
