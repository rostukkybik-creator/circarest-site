Refund Policy

Circarest · Last updated 11 November 2026

There's nothing to refund

Circarest is free, with no in-app purchases. You can't be charged for it, so there are no payments to refund and nothing to cancel.

We may offer paid features in a future version. If we do, they'll be sold and billed by Apple through the App Store, and Apple will process every payment and decide every refund under its own policies. Refunds would be requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We'll update this page before any paid feature launches.

Need help?

If something isn't working, email hello@5hz.io and we'll try to fix it. Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the law.
