Circarest

Sleep, light and caffeine planned around your shifts

Built for night shifts

Shift work asks a lot of your body. Circarest is a body-clock companion for nurses, paramedics, police, firefighters, factory and warehouse teams, and anyone working nights, 12-hour shifts or a rotating schedule. Add your shifts once; it suggests when to sleep, when to get bright light, when your last coffee should be, and when to eat. The plan updates as your schedule changes.

Coming soon to the App Store

Free to download, with an optional Circarest Plus subscription. No account, no ads, no tracking. Needs an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. For adults 18 and over.

Your day, planned around your shifts

Today shows what to do now and what's next. The Circadian Map lays out sleep, light and caffeine hour by hour, in plain English. Switching cards walk you from days to nights and back.

Time your caffeine around sleep

Your personal last-coffee time, worked out from what you've actually had, and an estimate of how much caffeine will still be active when you go to sleep.

Rest and recovery

A nap planner timed to end before you leave, wake alarms for daytime sleep, and a way to tell the people you live with when you're sleeping.

Check in before the drive home

A 90-second reaction-time check plus your hours awake and how sleepy you feel. Every check is saved with its date so you can compare over time. It looks for signs of fatigue; it can't tell you you're safe to drive.

Made for night work

Red night mode keeps the screen dim and red, which is easier on your eyes in the dark. Siri can answer "When's my caffeine cutoff in Circarest?" With Circarest Plus, widgets and a Live Activity keep the plan on your Lock Screen.

Private by design

Your data stays on your iPhone unless you choose to export or share it, and we never see it. Lock the app with Face ID, export everything as one file, or erase it all. See the Privacy Policy.

Circarest Plus

The Drive Check, wake alarms, the nap planner and your daily plan are free for everyone. Circarest Plus adds Learns Your Sleep, sleep debt and how to catch up, your plan in Apple Calendar, Snap photo recognition, weekly insights, Apple Health and wearables, a personal health plan, widgets and the Live Activity, calendar import, and 3 years of history (free keeps 6 months).

Circarest is free to download. Circarest Plus costs $5.99 a month or $39.99 a year (US prices), with no free trial. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account, and you can manage or cancel it any time in your Apple Account settings. See the Refund Policy.

Important

Circarest provides lifestyle and circadian suggestions for general wellness. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition, including sleep disorders. Talk to a doctor about ongoing sleep problems, loud snoring or daytime sleepiness. The Drive Check supports your own judgement; if you feel drowsy, don't drive.
