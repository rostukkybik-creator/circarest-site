Circarest

Sleep, light and caffeine planned around your shifts

Built for night shifts

Shift work asks a lot of your body. Circarest is a body-clock companion for nurses, paramedics, police, firefighters, factory and warehouse teams, and anyone working nights, 12-hour shifts or a rotating schedule. Add your shifts once; it plans when to sleep, when to get bright light, when your last coffee should be, and when to eat. Everything updates as your schedule changes.

Coming soon to the App Store

Free. No account, no ads, no tracking. iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Your day, planned around your shifts

Today shows what to do right now and what's next. The Circadian Map lays out sleep, light and caffeine hour by hour, in plain English. Switching cards walk you from days to nights and back.

Caffeine that doesn't cost you sleep

Your personal last-coffee time, worked out from what you've actually had, and how much caffeine will still be active when you go to sleep.

Rest and recovery

A nap planner timed to end before you leave, wake alarms for daytime sleep, and a way to tell the people you live with when you're sleeping.

Check in before the drive home

A 90-second reaction-time check plus your hours awake and how sleepy you feel. Every check is saved with its date so you can compare over time.

Made for night work

Red night mode protects your night vision. Widgets and a Live Activity keep the plan on your Lock Screen. Siri answers "When's my caffeine cutoff?"

Private by design

Your data stays on your iPhone, and you can lock the app with Face ID, export everything as one file, or erase it all. We never see it.

Important

This app provides lifestyle and circadian suggestions for general wellness and is not a regulated medical device or treatment for sleep disorders. Talk to a doctor about ongoing sleep problems, loud snoring or daytime sleepiness. The Safe to Drive check supports your own judgement; if you feel drowsy, don't drive.
